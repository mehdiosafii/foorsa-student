# Where the onboarding's recordings come from

Every recording here is from BigSoundBank (Joseph Sardin), released into the
public domain under CC0 1.0: free for any project, commercial or not, with no
attribution required. They are kept as FLAC so `compose.py` needs no network.

| File | BigSoundBank | Recording | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `paper-torn-off.flac` | [#3244, torn paper 7](https://bigsoundbank.com/torn-paper-7-s3244.html) | A sheet of paper torn off | the boarding pass's stub coming off (`tear`) |
| `instamatic-shutter.flac` | [#2822, Instamatic triggering 2](https://bigsoundbank.com/instamatic-triggering-2-s2822.html) | A Kodak Instamatic 133-X releasing its shutter | the scanner taking the passport page (`shutter`) |
| `rope-whoosh.flac` | [#1796, whoosh 4](https://bigsoundbank.com/whoosh-4-s1796.html) | A long rope swung through the air | the arrow's flight (`swoop`) and the plane's climb (`intro`) |
| `gong-sweet.flac` | [#1482, gong, sweet](https://bigsoundbank.com/gong-sweet-s1482.html) | A soft mallet on a small hanging gong (C sharp, 20 cents sharp) | the start of each loop of the bed, tuned to C sharp (`bed`) |
| `small-ratchet.flac` | [#0794, small ratchet](https://bigsoundbank.com/small-ratchet-s0794.html), 2.0 to 6.0 s | A small ratchet's teeth | the departures board's flaps, re-timed into a settling board (`flap`) |
